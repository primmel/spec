[[annex-f-lml-alignment]]
== Annex F (normative): The LML alignment — the strict-superset proof

Primmel's data-modelling substrate IS the LutaML Model Language (LML,
RS 3001 + RS 3010); Primmel strictly extends it with the metrology
loop's higher constructs. This annex is the proof table: every LML
construct, its Primmel counterpart, and its status. A status of
*declined* carries its reason and is final until this annex is
revised. The registry's Basis column (<<annex-a-constructs,Annex A>>)
carries the same alignment per construct.

[cols="2,2,2,3", options="header"]
|===
|LML construct |LML ref |Primmel counterpart |Status

|package / models scope |lang §Package |`package` (<<model-structure,9>>) |aligned (v2)
|class |lang §Class |`class` |aligned (v2; inherit)
|attribute (+ cardinality) |lang §Attribute |`datum` (<<subject-model,10.2>>) |unified this revision (was attribute_definition/identity_slot/parameter/field)
|attribute `default` + nil policy |decl §Attribute defaults |datum facets `default`, `render_nil` |adopted this revision
|instance-type override `isa` |decl §isa |`instance` `of` + declared override |adopted this revision (the `of` slot accepts an override entry)
|value types |lang §Value |`value_types` plane |aligned (v3)
|enums |lang §Enums |`enum` |aligned (v2; inherit)
|enum member payloads `member_fields` |decl §Enum member payloads |enum `member_fields` + inline member payloads |adopted this revision
|table-driven enums `from_table` |decl §Table-driven enums |enum `from_table` (compile-time expansion from a grammar artifact's tables) |adopted this revision

|===

The higher constructs — subjects, promises, requirements, processes
with step signatures, conformance tests and runs, evidence, forms,
attestation — have NO LML counterpart: they are the strictly-greater
side of the relationship (<<requirements-and-conformance,clauses 11>>
to <<attestation,20>>).
