[[annex-f-lml-alignment]]
== Annex F (normative): The LML alignment — the strict-superset proof

Primmel's data-modelling substrate IS the LutaML Model Language (LML,
RS 3001 + RS 3010); Primmel strictly extends it with the metrology
loop's higher constructs. This annex is the proof table: every LML
construct, its Primmel counterpart, and its status. A status of
*declined* carries its reason and is final until this annex is
revised. The registry's Basis column (<<annex-a-constructs,Annex A>>)
carries the same alignment per construct. Each row names the
implemented carrier or the recorded refusal; the LML-alignment
register (TODO.lang-gaps/09–19) carries the full disposition and the
reopen trigger, and is the authority this table is reconciled to
(the 2026-10-28 audit).

The higher constructs — subjects, promises, requirements, processes
with step signatures, conformance tests and runs, evidence, forms,
attestation — have NO LML counterpart: they are the strictly-greater
side of the relationship (<<requirements-and-conformance,clauses 11>>
to <<attestation,20>>).
