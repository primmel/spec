== Annex E (normative): The v2 bridge — where every construct lives

This annex is the bridge the reconfiguration promises: for every
construct of the parser, it states where its normative definition
lives, and what status the v2 generation's documents carry. The
table is generated from <<annex-a-constructs,Annex A>>'s registry
rows; it is regenerated when the registry regenerates, and it is
never hand-edited.

The rule the bridge states is the one-home rule: the language part
of this specification is normative for every construct's definition,
and the v2 generation's supporting parts — the data-model,
process-model, compliance, measurement, calculations, forms,
symbols, terminology, workspace, mapping and state-machines parts,
shared byte for byte with the MMEL v2 record — remain published as
the record of the v2 generation. Where a supporting part and this
specification overlap, this specification governs; a supporting
part's statement of a construct's grammar is the v2 record, not a
second normative home.

The constructs of the v3 generations (v3, v3.1, v3.2) and the
platform modules of <<platform-modules,clause 20>> are specified in
this specification directly; they have no v2 home. The constructs
the parser carries but Annex A does not yet register carry their
triage dispositions in the kernel repository's visible
`registry-triage.prl` list under the construction freeze
(<<annex-a-constructs,Annex A>>'s preamble states the freeze).
